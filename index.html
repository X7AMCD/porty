About

My Story

Hi, I'm Your Name

I didn't grow up with a camera in my hands. My first "camera" was a cracked phone and a stubborn habit of noticing things: the way late-afternoon light slid across a classroom wall, the half-second when a friend forgot to pose and simply laughed.

Then I saved up for a second-hand camera and a single lens, and I took it everywhere. I photographed friends on weekends, strangers on street corners and my family at every gathering, until they stopped complaining. I was learning in public, one blurry frame at a time, and I kept every picture that taught me something.

Slowly, people started asking, "Can you do that for me?" My first paid shoot was a friend's graduation portraits, and I was so nervous my hands shook. When she saw the photos she cried, and that was the moment I understood this was more than a hobby.

"I'm not chasing perfect poses. I'm chasing the moment you forget I'm there."

Today I'm still early in this journey, and I'm proud of that. Every shoot makes me sharper and every client teaches me something new. I bring fresh energy, a love of natural light and a promise that you'll feel comfortable from the first minute to the last.

If you want photographs that feel like you instead of a template, I'd love to meet you.
